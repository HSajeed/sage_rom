Sign convention (double-checked, see UEqn.H comment history / commit
message): `+ fvm::Sp(cD*mag(U), U)` sits on the LHS of
`tUEqn == fvOptions(U)`, i.e. inside the momentum matrix being solved as
`ddt(U) + div(phi,U) + MRF.DDt(U) + divDevReff(U) + cD*mag(U)*U ==
fvOptions(U)`, and momentum is then solved as
`UEqn == -fvc::grad(p)`. Moving everything but ddt(U) to the RHS:
`dU/dt = -div(phi,U) - MRF.DDt(U) - divDevReff(U) + fvOptions(U)
         - grad(p) - cD*mag(U)*U`
so the added term contributes `-cD*mag(U)*U`, a drag SINK on the momentum
RHS for cD > 0 -- matching the physical `-cD|U|U` quadratic drag the term
is meant to represent, not a source.
